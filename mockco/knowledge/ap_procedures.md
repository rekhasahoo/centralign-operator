# Accounts Payable: Invoice Entry Procedure (Northwind Traders)

1. **Amount to record** is the invoice's final payable total *including* GST/tax, never the subtotal.
2. Enter amounts as plain numbers: no commas, no currency symbols (e.g. 62400.00).
3. Enter dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Slash-formatted dates on vendor invoices are day/month/year (India).
4. "Latest invoice" means the most recent *invoice date*, not file name or file modified time.
5. Use the invoice number printed on the document. If the document shows a PO number, it MUST be entered in the PO field.
6. Any invoice over INR 50,000 must be approved by the requester before it is submitted.
7. Vendor names in the portal: Acme Corp, Globex Ltd, Initech.
8. Never submit an invoice number that already exists; check the Invoices list first.
